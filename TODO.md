# Devfolio — Coursework Outcomes

- [x] **Responsive portfolio presentation**
  - The site introduces the developer, states ambitions, and uses clear navigation across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  - Interactive elements are keyboard-friendly and show readable focus states.

- [x] **Profile and contact content**
  - The portfolio includes a developer introduction, career ambitions, contact information, and a prominent LinkedIn profile link.
  - Personal details that were not supplied are clearly labeled as editable sample content.

- [x] **Education and certifications**
  - The portfolio lists qualifications, institutions or issuers, dates, and relevant context.

- [x] **Projects and skills**
  - Multiple projects are showcased with descriptions, individual roles, technologies used, and measurable or clearly stated outcomes.
  - Technical skills are separated from other relevant professional and communication skills.

- [x] **Experience, credibility, writing, and achievements**
  - Work history details employers, positions, dates, responsibilities, and contributions.
  - Testimonials are included when available, with editable sample placeholders where source material is unavailable.
  - Blog/articles links demonstrate technical knowledge and communication skills.
  - Awards and honors are featured.

- [x] **Functional to-do list**
  - Users can create, edit, complete, filter, and delete tasks.
  - Tasks persist in browser local storage without sign-in, backend infrastructure, or external credentials.
